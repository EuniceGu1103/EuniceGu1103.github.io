About 关于

I'm Eunice, an undergraduate at Duke Kunshan University and Duke University (Class of 2027), majoring in Applied Mathematics and Computational Science on the mathematics track.

I work at the intersection of climate and health — using statistics and machine learning to understand how extreme weather affects people, especially children. When I'm not with data, I'm usually behind a camera; I served as Vice President of Pixel Poets, my university's photography club.

I like problems that sit between disciplines: rigorous enough to model, human enough to matter.

Research 研究

Two questions I keep returning to

01

Climate Science气候科学

Climate modeling

Exploring the underlying principles and applied logic of numerical weather prediction (NWP) and climate-projection models — including work on Southern Hemisphere jet variability and eddy–mean flow interactions using ERA5 reanalysis.

Extreme weather events

Analyzing how extreme weather events form, evolve, and compound — and what those dynamics mean for the systems and communities in their path.

02

Public Health公共卫生

Climate & health

Investigating the health risks of climate variability in low- and middle-income countries, with a focus on the uneven burden it places on children — particularly respiratory and diarrheal disease.

Data-driven decisions

Combining statistical modeling and machine learning across large-scale datasets (DHS surveys, ERA5) to surface nonlinear climate–disease relationships that inform real decisions.
